What We're About

Train Hard. Roll Smart. Grow Together.

BJJ at UCI is a student-run club open to anyone curious about Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu — whether you've never set foot on a mat or you're already competing. Classes focus on fundamentals, live rolling, and building a community that pushes each other to improve.

No gi? No problem. New to campus? Even better. We keep the environment welcoming for first-timers while still giving more experienced grapplers real work on the mats.

Why Join?

  • Beginner-friendly instruction — no experience or gi required to start.
  • A consistent group to drill and roll with, every week.
  • A community on and off the mats — socials, meetups, and travel to tournaments.
